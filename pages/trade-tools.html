Trade Tools

Find realistic trades and evaluate proposed ones, based on rest-of-season value and each team's own positional needs - not just value in a vacuum.

Show methodology
How suggestions are scored: mostly whether each side's roster is actually better as a whole - starters and bench compared before vs. after the trade, market-value adjusted so a QB upgrade cannot look like a bigger deal than it really is. The points/week and rank numbers shown are real projections, not the score itself. If a trade would leave a lineup spot empty, it names exactly who to pick up from waivers to fill it.
Show methodology
How Fairness is scored: half raw value parity, half whether each side's roster is actually better as a whole (market-value adjusted, same as the Finder) - the points/week and rank numbers shown are real projections, not the score itself, plus who to add from waivers if it leaves a lineup spot empty.
