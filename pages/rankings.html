Team Rankings

Every manager's individual season, ranked against every other season in league history and adjusted for each year's scoring environment.

How seasons are compared

Each row is one manager's single season, not a career average. Scoring rules changed over the years, so every season's league-wide average is compared with the all-time average to get a year factor. Adjusted PPG = raw PPG × that year's factor. Opponent PPG (regular season only) is how many points a manager's opponents scored, adjusted the same way.

Ranks are always against the full population of seasons, so a filtered view keeps each season's true all-time rank. On the Playoffs tab, the pill compares a season's playoff rank with its regular-season rank: green when the team played better in the playoffs.

Bars show each season's factor. Red = a high-scoring year (scores adjusted down), green = a low-scoring year (adjusted up).
