Transactions

Every waiver claim, free agent pickup and trade since 2013, including the losing FAAB bids.

All completed trades that could be reliably extracted from the league records. No trade records exist for 2013-2021 (not tracked that way back then). 2022 dates come from ESPN's trade log (the day each trade was accepted); 2023-2025 dates are from the year-end reports; 2026 trades come straight from ESPN and update automatically.

How trades are graded

What counts. Every player a team received is followed from the week the trade went through (read from ESPN's weekly rosters) to the end of that season, for as long as that team kept him. Weeks after he was dropped or traded away don't count. Points are real NWL scoring from ESPN box scores.

Points over replacement. Each week a player earns his points minus what a waiver-level player at his position scored that week (the average of the players ranked just past the league's starters among everyone rostered: 13th–18th at QB, TE and HC, 31st–36th at RB and WR), never below zero. So adding bodies doesn't win a trade by itself. Fantasy playoff weeks (15–17) count 1.5× because they decide titles.

Grades. Each side's total minus the other side's: A+ at +80 or more, A +50, A− +30, B+ +15, B within 15 of even, C down to −40, D down to −80, F beyond. Started points (what the manager actually put in his lineup) are shown but don't change the grade. FAAB dollars and draft picks in a trade are listed, not graded. A trade less than 30 days old is marked too early to grade.

Coverage. ESPN box scores are only available to the site's ESPN account from 2022 on, so trades before 2022 can't be graded. Two 2022 trades (Prodahl–Stover and Hagan–Goetz) were recorded with the sides swapped; ESPN's rosters show every player moving the other way, so they were corrected.
